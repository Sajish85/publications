Fault Injection and Verification for Lakehouse Table Maintenance: Crash-Testing Apache Iceberg Compaction, Snapshot Expiry and Orphan Removal

Sajish Krishnan · Independent researcher · ORCID: 0009-0009-7561-9290
Published 2026-11-20 · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24412833 · Licence: CC BY 4.0

Abstract

Apache Iceberg tables need regular maintenance: small-file compaction (rewrite_data_files), snapshot expiry (expire_snapshots) and orphan-file removal (remove_orphan_files). These operations delete or replace files that readers and writers depend on, while ingestion continues. I built a fault-injection (chaos engineering) checker that runs each operation against a live ingest, kills or pauses maintenance at chosen phases, then checks the table against a ledger of acknowledged writes. The checker scores three classes separately: A (data integrity), B (retained snapshots and tags), and C (whether a maintenance call's reported progress matches what committed). An independent reader (pyiceberg) repeats the A and B checks on every cell. I ran 123 cells on three builds (1.7.1, 1.12.0, and main at commit 2d89aa4): 369 cells in total. Classes A and B recorded no violation in any of the 369 cells; the two readers never disagreed. I found no novel violation. The only class C flags map to an existing, open issue (#17202): a partial-progress rewrite returns success while only some commits land after a concurrent expiry removes its starting snapshot. The signature appeared in 8 of 8 in-matrix race cells on 1.7.1. A gated re-run forcing real overlap between the expiry commit and the rewrite's commits flagged 3 of 3 cells on 1.7.1, 8 of 8 on 1.12.0 and 7 of 8 on main. Reported counts were exact in every flagged cell; only failed_data_files_count = 0 misleads. A positive control for live-file deletion (#17304, closed as spec-discouraged and contested) is detected by classes A and B on all three builds through the core API, but not through the Spark procedure or on a tagged table. The negative result is narrow: it covers an unpartitioned, append-only, tagged table, a Postgres JDBC catalog and single-node MinIO, and cannot speak to the phases my rig never reached.
